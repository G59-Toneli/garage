# Manual do Proprietário — Chevrolet Kadett GSi (1993)

*Invented document. Not a real General Motors publication. All figures are fictional.*

## Tyres and pressures

The GSi leaves the factory on 185/60 R14 tyres. Cold inflation pressure is 28 psi at the front and
26 psi at the rear for normal loads. With four occupants and luggage, raise the rear to 32 psi. The
spare wheel is stored inflated to 41 psi and must be corrected before extended use.

## Maintenance intervals

Take the car to a workshop every 10 000 km or twelve months, whichever comes first. The timing belt
is a replacement item at 60 000 km; it is not a lifetime component, and a broken belt on this engine
destroys valves.

## Warning lamps

The engine management lamp is amber and shaped like an engine block. If it stays lit while driving,
the car may be used at reduced load to reach a workshop. The oil pressure lamp is red. If it lights
while the engine is running, stop immediately and do not restart.

## Fuel

Use gasolina comum. The engine management system will tolerate álcool blends up to 25% without
adjustment, but fuel consumption rises.
