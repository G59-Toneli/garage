## Section 4.1 — Procedure: removing the camshaft

1. Disconnect the battery negative lead and wait ten minutes before touching the ignition harness.
2. Remove the camshaft cover and set the crankshaft to top dead centre on cylinder one.
3. Slacken the timing belt tensioner and lift the belt clear of the camshaft sprocket.
4. Loosen the five camshaft bearing cap nuts a quarter turn at a time, working from cap 5 to cap 1.
5. Lift the camshaft out squarely. Do not lever against the bearing surfaces.
6. On reassembly, tighten the bearing cap nuts to 23 N·m in the order 1 to 5.
